 KEY THRESHOLDS AT A GLANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

 INTEREST RATES
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Fed Funds Rate:
   > 5.0%  = Restrictive    2.5-5.0% = Neutral     < 2.5% = Accommodative

 10-Year Treasury:
   > 4.5%  = High           3.0-4.5% = Normal      < 3.0% = Low

 Yield Curve (10Y - 2Y):
   < 0 bp  = INVERTED (Recession signal!)
   0-50 bp = Flat           > 50 bp  = Normal

 REAL RATES (the most important price in capitalism)
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Real Fed Funds (Fed Funds - CPI):
   > 2.0%  = Very Tight     0-2.0%   = Tight
   -1-0%   = Neutral        < -1.0%  = Loose

 Real 10Y (TIPS):
   > 2.0%  = Very Tight     0.5-2.0% = Tight
   -0.5-0.5% = Neutral      < -0.5%  = Loose

 INFLATION
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 CPI Year-over-Year:
   > 5.0%  = Very High      3.0-5.0% = Elevated
   2.0-3.0% = Normal        < 2.0%   = Low

 Core PCE (Fed's preferred):
   > 4.0%  = Very High      2.5-4.0% = Above Target
   1.5-2.5% = At Target     < 1.5%   = Below Target

 CPI Month-over-Month:
   > 0.4%  = Hot            0.2-0.4% = Warm        < 0.2% = Cool

 5Y5Y Forward Inflation:
   > 3.0%  = Unanchored     2.0-3.0% = Anchored    < 2.0% = Unanchored Low

 CREDIT SPREADS
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 High Yield OAS:
   > 600bp = STRESS         400-600bp = Elevated
   300-400bp = Normal       < 300bp   = Tight (complacent)

 Investment Grade OAS:
   > 200bp = STRESS         130-200bp = Elevated
   80-130bp = Normal        < 80bp    = Tight

 TED Spread:
   > 100bp = Banking Stress  50-100bp = Elevated    < 50bp = Normal

 LABOR MARKET
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Unemployment:
   > 6.0%  = Recession      5.0-6.0% = Weakening
   3.5-5.0% = Normal        < 3.5%   = Tight

 Nonfarm Payrolls (monthly change):
   < 0k    = CONTRACTION    0-100k   = Slowing
   100-250k = Normal        > 250k   = Strong

 Initial Claims:
   > 300k  = Recession Risk  > 250k  = Elevated    < 250k = Normal

 GROWTH
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 GDP (QoQ annualized):
   < 0%    = CONTRACTION    0-1.0%   = Stall Speed
   1.0-3.0% = Normal        > 3.0%   = Strong

 ISM Manufacturing & Services PMI:
   < 45    = Deep Contraction   45-50 = Contraction
   50-55   = Expansion          > 55  = Strong Expansion

 MARKET
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 VIX:
   > 30    = FEAR           20-30    = Elevated
   12-20   = Normal         < 12     = Complacent
